# Roadmap and vision

Internal. Last updated: 2026-11-03. Each phase is earned by the one before it. Phase contents are proposed (2026-10-29, 2026-11-02) unless marked; Krischan set the overall order (browser, extension, mobile, maybe Discord) and parked the streamer side.

## The opportunity

VTuber fans already watch together in Discord calls and group chats, but nothing makes them feel like they're sitting in the same room while staying quiet. A light, cute, fan-made window that respects old hardware and never uses AI art can spread through fandoms quickly, starting in the Philippines.

## v0: sit together (browser)

See [scope.md](scope.md). **Proves:** friends keep the window open for a whole stream.

## v1: the extension

- Chat flying up from your party's seats (sender's own extension announces it).
- Typing bubbles.
- Automatic stream detection; video ID and live state read from the page, never written to it.
- Oshi mark picked up from your display name.
- Chrome, Edge and Firefox (test Firefox's background-script model separately).

**Proves:** the extension is worth installing over the website.

## v1.5: watching alone

- Nearby panel, waving, and cards with links to known platforms, shown on mutual wave.
- This is what unlocks people without friends on the app. (Krischan called connections "the core of the application", 2026-10-29)

## v2: reactions and fandom

- Superchats and akasupa shown in the theater (detected locally).
- Penlights in the oshi's color; other reactions; bursts merged.
- Fan-drawn oshi marks, credited and reviewed.
- Accounts when purchases or lasting friend lists need them.
- Cosmetics by fan artists; tip jar and supporters wall already live since v0.

## v3: streamers (parked for a long time)

- A read-only theater page for OBS as a browser source.
- Streamer-controlled reactions (tomatoes on or off).
- Analytics with the streamer's own sign-in.

## Other directions

- Mobile: Android overlay app; Safari web extension on iPhone and iPad; iOS can't overlay the YouTube app.
- Discord Activity, if Discord allows it.
- Twitch, if the first partner streams there.
- VCT as a use case (team marks, two cheering sides).
- Custom avatars, editable by artists (v2 or v3).

## Watch-outs

- Scope grows fast. Keep each phase to its one question.
- Any AI art reaching the public would sink trust with the audience.
- Corporate VTuber fan-work rules: never sell anything based on their talent.
- YouTube can change its page at any time; the extension reads as little as possible from it.
