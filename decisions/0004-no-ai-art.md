# 0004 — No AI-generated art

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Context

Krischan never uses AI-generated graphics or design for anyone (standing rule). VTuber and oshi-based fandoms strongly reject AI art (Krischan, 2026-10-29), so any AI art in the product would cost the audience's trust.

## Decision

No AI-generated graphics, sprites, avatars, emoji, icons or illustrations ship in the product or its marketing.

- Oshi marks are the device's own emoji, drawn by Apple's, Google's, Microsoft's or Samsung's designers. If pixelated, it's by a fixed, non-AI algorithm on the device ([research](../research/2026-10-29-oshi-mark-pixelation.md)). Fan-drawn marks come later, credited.
- Avatars and themes come from human artists, credited and paid where sold.
- Krischan rejected AI-produced emoji and pixel packs (2026-10-29). Existing human-made sets were looked at (SerenityOS emoji, R74moji) but not adopted ([research](../research/2026-10-29-platform-costs-and-assets.md)).

## Known placeholders that must be replaced

The mockups made on 2026-10-29 contain AI-made placeholder art, drawn in code: the chibi party avatars (yuzu, kuma, hoshi, ren, hikari), the star on the stage, the curtains, and the pixel icons. None of it ships. v0 avoids illustrated art entirely so nothing blocks launch.

## Code

The pixelation code and mockup code were written by an AI. That is not art, but if the team wants to claim "no AI anywhere", a person rewrites that code first. Otherwise the public promise is "no AI art". Krischan decides the wording ([open questions](../project/open-questions.md)).

## Alternatives considered

- AI-produced pixel emoji packs: rejected; the fandom would not accept them.
