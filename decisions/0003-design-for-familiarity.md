# 0003 — Design for familiarity

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Decided by:** Krischan (standing default from the team's template, adapted)
**Status:** accepted

## Context

Krischan's standing design philosophy is Jakob's law: people spend most of their time on other sites and apps, and expect ours to work the same way. This product has a strong costume (the DS settings screen), which could tempt us into clever controls.

## Decision

The DS look is the costume; the behavior underneath is familiar.

- Buttons have words on them. Icon-only controls get labels for screen readers.
- The main action is where people look for it: joining on the site, "Copy invite" in the window.
- Touch targets at least 44px.
- The landing page follows normal web patterns: visible navigation, a clear main button, an FAQ.
- Fandom vocabulary (oshi, akasupa, penlight) is familiar to this audience, so use it.

## Why

A fan who just wants to sit with friends should get there without learning anything. Charm comes from the look and the small moments, not from making people work.
