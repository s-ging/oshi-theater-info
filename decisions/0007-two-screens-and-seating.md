# 0007 — Two screens, straight-row seating, chat that rises behind the frame

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Context

The original vision was a single tilted theater with a bird's-eye view and a zoomed close-up. Three rounds of mockups on 2026-10-29 settled the layout.

## Decision

- **Two screens like a DS.** Top: the theater. Bottom: your party up close. Krischan: "it's almost impossible not to visualize it that way."
- **Stage across the full width** of the top screen. No exit signs.
- **Seating:** 6 rows, each two blocks of 6 seats with an aisle; rounded squares in straight rows. 72 seats per theater.
- **Chat flying up:** a party member's message rises straight up from their seat on the bottom screen, passes behind the DS frame at the hinge, comes out on the top screen, then fades over a random amount of time.

## Tried and rejected

- **Circle seats, curved rows, staggered rows** (second mockup round): Krischan: "it looks really eh, because we're doing too many things at a time." Do not bring these back without asking.
- **Chat flying diagonally to land above the friend's seat in the theater**: replaced by the straight rise and fade.
- **Square seats with sharp corners** (first round): replaced by rounded squares.
- **CSS 3D tilt** from the original handoff: blurs pixel art.

## Open

- An optional "depth" variant (front rows slightly bigger) is in the mockup as a switch, off by default. Undecided.
- Keep 72 seats per theater, or the original 100?
