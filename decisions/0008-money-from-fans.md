# 0008 — Money comes from fans: no ads, no VTuber revenue split

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Decided by:** Krischan
**Status:** accepted

## Context

Servers aren't free, and Krischan doesn't want to pay out of pocket for long. Fans mostly want to spend on their oshi, not on a tool.

## Decision

- No ads, even though they're the easiest option.
- No revenue split with VTubers. Krischan: "nigh impossible", especially with corporate VTubers, who wouldn't want to take part.
- The money comes from fans, transparently, so they can choose to support it. (Krischan, 2026-11-02)

Pathways, the "never charge for" list and the transparency plan are in [product/monetization.md](../product/monetization.md). Those are proposed, not yet confirmed.

## Alternatives considered

- Ads: rejected.
- Official theater kits with VTubers and a revenue split: rejected as unrealistic.
