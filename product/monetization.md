# Monetization

Last updated: 2026-11-03. Krischan wants to be transparent with users so they can support the product. (2026-11-02)

## Fixed

Stated by Krischan, 2026-10-29. See [decision 0008](../decisions/0008-money-from-fans.md).

- No ads, even though they're the easiest money.
- No revenue split with VTubers. Corporate VTubers won't take part in something like that, so it's unrealistic.
- The money comes from fans, and it has to feel like it supports the fandom, because fans want to spend on their oshi.

## Never charge for

- The social core: sitting together, party size, waving, presence.
- Ads, or selling anyone's data.
- Gacha or loot boxes; many users are minors.
- Anything based on a specific VTuber's likeness or marks (corporate fan-work rules forbid selling it). Fan-drawn oshi marks stay free.

## Pathways, in launch order

Proposed by an agent, 2026-10-29 and 2026-11-02. Not yet confirmed.

1. **Tip jar from day one** (Ko-fi or similar). Check which platforms actually pay out in the Philippines: PayPal-based ones generally do; Stripe does not take Philippine accounts, as far as we know. Verify before choosing.
2. **Supporters wall:** supporters' names in the credits on the site, optionally a small trim on their seat. A thank-you, not an advantage.
3. **Cosmetics drawn by fan artists** (after the extension ships): seat skins, avatar frames, theater themes. Original art only. Show the split on each item, ideally with the artist getting most of it.
4. **Fan-project theaters:** a fan club books a decorated private theater for an oshi's birthday or anniversary stream. It costs more server time, so charging is fair, and the group pays, not one person.
5. **Streamer tier, much later:** the OBS view (parked).

Dropped: official theater kits made with VTubers with a revenue split (Krischan, 2026-10-29: "nigh impossible", especially with corporate VTubers).

## Transparency

Proposed 2026-11-02:

- A public "where the money goes" page, updated monthly: the server bill, tips received, and where the rest went.
- A fixed spending order, stated up front. Example: servers first, then commissioning fan artists for free cosmetics everyone gets, then the team's time.
- A "free forever" list on the site.
- Say who we are: a small team in the Philippines who watch streams too.

## Costs

Small. Cloudflare's free plan covers a soft launch; a busy first month is estimated at $5 to $20 (see [architecture.md](architecture.md#costs)). The real cost is the team's time.

## Open

- Should tips pay for the team's time, or only for servers and artists? Both are honest but need different wording ("help keep the lights on" vs "support the people making this"). Krischan to decide.
- Which tip platform pays out in the Philippines.
