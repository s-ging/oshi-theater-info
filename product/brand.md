# Brand

Last updated: 2026-11-03.

## Look

- The Nintendo DS settings screen: grid paper, white panels, chunky pixel type, small icons. It should run like it belongs on the DS, without the slog. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- Two screens with a hinge between them. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- Copy the mood, not Nintendo's actual icons, layout or trademarks. That matters once this is public. (agent note, 2026-10-29)

## What the mockups use (placeholders until a human designer confirms)

- **Pixel font:** DotGothic16 (Google Fonts, free, includes Japanese characters).
- **Body font on the landing page:** M PLUS Rounded 1c (Google Fonts).
- **Colors:** paper `#F3F3F6`, grid lines `#E3E4EA`, panels `#FFFFFF`, ink `#33333D`, muted text `#55586A`. You: gold `#F2C14E` with `#A5701A` border. Party: mint `#5CC8AE` with `#23806C` border. Stage curtain maroon `#6E2B45`. The stage takes the oshi's color.

## No AI art

See [decision 0004](../decisions/0004-no-ai-art.md). The sprites, star and curtains in the mockups were generated in code by an AI and are placeholders only. Avatars and marks come from human artists, credited. The landing page promise "drawn by people, no AI art" depends on keeping this rule.

## Copy

- Write like a person in the fandom talking to a friend. Plain, short sentences.
- Never generic AI-sounding copy: no grand label-plus-tagline pairs like "Home — Your window to the world", no "all in one place", "on your time", "you control who sees…", "one-of-a-kind personalized feed". (Krischan, stated as a standing rule)
- Use the fandom's own words where they fit: oshi, oshi mark, akasupa, penlight, 草.
- Until the product is named, write "[app name]".
- Mixing a little Japanese is fine when it's natural (一緒に見よう, "let's watch together"). Check anything new with Krischan's cousin.

## Promises to keep honest

The landing page mockup makes these. Each is Krischan's to confirm before launch:

- "No AI art, now or later."
- "Sitting with your friends is free."
- If code is written with AI help, say "no AI art", not "no AI anywhere". (agent note, 2026-10-29)
