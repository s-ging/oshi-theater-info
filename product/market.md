# Market

Audience, fandom culture, and where to launch. Last updated: 2026-11-03.

## Audience

- VTuber fans first: a large market. Krischan wants the product to spread between the Nijisanji (2434) and Hololive fanbases, Japanese and English. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- Friend groups who watch together, and people watching alone. (see [overview](overview.md))
- Many fans are teenagers. Design for that ([safety.md](safety.md)).
- VCT (Valorant esports) viewers are a second use case Krischan cares about; he watches VCT himself. (2026-10-29)

## Fandom culture to build on

- **Oshi:** your favorite streamer. **Oshi mark:** the emoji fans put after their names to show whose fan they are.
- **Superchats and akasupa** (red, top-tier superchats) are a big moment in YouTube streams. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- **Penlights:** waving glowsticks in the oshi's color, from idol and VTuber concert culture. (agent note)
- **Fan projects:** fans raise money and organize for birthdays and debut anniversaries. (agent note)
- **AI art is rejected outright** by these fandoms. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- Fans want to support their oshi, not a tool. Money has to feel like it goes back to the fandom. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)

## Launch strategy

- Local (Philippines) first, then scale. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- A partnership with a local VTuber would be a strong start and spread between communities. Krischan is not set on specific VTubers; collab-willing VTubers are easy to find. (2026-10-29)
- Agent notes (2026-10-29):
  - Start with independent VTubers: they can say yes on the spot. Corporate talent (Hololive, Nijisanji) goes through the agency and has strict fan-work guidelines.
  - Bring a working beta they can try, not a pitch deck.
  - Check which platform the first partner streams on. If it's Twitch, Twitch support moves into v1.
  - Organic discovery first; the original handoff planned no streamer outreach at launch.

## VCT as a test case

Agent notes (2026-10-29):

- Team marks instead of oshi marks; the theater can split into two cheering sides.
- Big moments (a clutch, an ace) could set off crowd reactions.
- Big VCT streams have huge audiences, which tests splitting a stream into many theaters early.
- Krischan watches alone, so it is a natural test of the watching-alone side.

## Around us

- **Discord:** where friend groups already talk and watch together. We add to it, not compete. A Discord Activity is a possible later step.
- **YouTube live chat:** we read it (with the extension), never replace it.
- **Streamer-facing overlay and chat tools:** some already exist. We are different because we are fan-facing and fan-made, connecting fans to streamers. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
