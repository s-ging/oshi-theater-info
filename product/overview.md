# Overview

Last updated: 2026-11-03.

Each line says whether Krischan stated it or an agent proposed it. Proposals are not decisions until Krischan confirms them.

## What it is

- A place to watch your oshi with your friends, sitting beside them silently. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- It feels like couch co-op multiplayer while watching your favorite streamer. Making friend groups and hanging out is the point. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- It adds to watching together; it does not compete with Discord. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- A separate window next to the stream that visualizes the seating. It never draws inside YouTube's page. (Krischan, 2026-10-29; [decision 0005](../decisions/0005-window-beside-the-stream.md))
- The window has two screens, like a DS: the theater on top, your party on the bottom. (Krischan liked it, 2026-10-29; [decision 0007](../decisions/0007-two-screens-and-seating.md))
- Fan-made and fan-facing. Streamers can choose to show it on stream one day, but it is the audience's tool first. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)

## Who it is for

- The VTuber fandom first, because it is a large market. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- Friend groups who already watch together, and people watching alone who want company. Watching alone matters from day one: it fills the theater so a new user never walks into an empty room. (proposed 2026-10-29; Krischan agreed that connections for solo watchers are "the core of the application")
- Krischan also watches VCT (Valorant esports) streams and is curious about that use case. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)

## Where it launches

- Local first (Philippines), then scale. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- Browser-first: open the site, enter a stream link or a friend's party code. The browser extension is the upgrade. (Krischan, 2026-10-29; [decision 0006](../decisions/0006-browser-first.md))

## Expansion order

Stated by Krischan, 2026-10-29:

1. Website (browser version)
2. Browser extension (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
3. A lightweight mobile overlay app, for people who watch on tablets or phones
4. Possibly a Discord extension, if Discord allows it

Long term, streamers could add it to OBS or their streaming software and see their audience as a crowd. Parked "for very long". (Krischan, 2026-10-29)

## Name

- Not "Pixel Theater"; that was a working name from an earlier handoff. (Krischan, 2026-10-29)
- Undecided. Krischan will settle it with his cousin, who studies international studies (Japanese) and is in the VTuber and anime sphere. Ideas Krischan floated: OshiMite, MiteOshi, Oshiomiru, Issho-oshi. (2026-10-29)
- Note from an agent: in everyday Japanese 付き合う (tsukiau) mostly means "to date someone", so avoid names built on it. 一緒 (issho, "together") has no such problem. Confirm with the cousin.
