# Safety

Last updated: 2026-11-03. VTuber fandoms include many minors and have real harassment and doxxing problems. Everything here is proposed by an agent (2026-10-29 to 2026-11-02) unless marked; Krischan has not objected, but confirm before treating any of it as final.

## v0 (browser version)

- **Strangers are anonymous.** You see names only for your own party. Everyone else in the theater is a colored seat. This removes most moderation work at launch.
- **No links, no free text to strangers.** Reactions are a fixed set of buttons.
- **Limits:** display name length, rate limits on reactions and joins, a kick button for the party host.
- **Nothing stored:** a name and emoji while seated, forgotten when the room empties. Say so in a short privacy note.

## Connections and waving (later)

- Your card shows to your party automatically, and to a stranger only after you both wave.
- Links only to known platforms (X, Bluesky, Twitch, YouTube, Discord, Pixiv and similar), never any URL, so a scam link can't reach a teenager through a stranger's card.
- "Hide my card" at any time.
- The card can live only on your device and be sent while you're seated, so the server never stores profiles.
- Conversation moves to the platforms people already use. We don't build direct messages, which would mean moderation, reporting and responsibility for minors. (Krischan's own idea was to "message somewhere else", 2026-10-29)

## Reactions shown to streamers (later)

- Once streamers can show the theater on stream, a reaction thrown at the oshi becomes something a raid can abuse.
- Positive and silly reactions by default (penlights, claps). Tomatoes only where the streamer turns them on.
- Bursts merge: 30 tomatoes become one big splat.

## Fan-drawn marks and cosmetics (later)

- Every submission reviewed before it goes live.
- Written permission from each artist (a checkbox on submission at minimum).
- Free marks only; never sell anything based on a VTuber's likeness.

## Payments (later)

- No gacha or loot boxes.
- Use payment platforms that handle age and refunds.
